Digital marketing

Every channel gets one clear job.

A · Channel audit and discoverability
  • Review Instagram and Facebook posts, format mix, activity and available analytics.
  • Test the website on mobile: services, hours, address and contact paths.
  • Check the Google Business Profile (if owned or eligible): pin, hours, contact.
  • Compare a few nearby competitors' public positioning, with no impersonation or fake inquiries.
  • Align the clinic name, location and main contact across channels.
C · Contact-experience improvements
  • Map the journey from profile visit or search to an administrative inquiry.
  • Suggest approved profile links and contact-button fixes.
  • Draft administrative WhatsApp quick replies: location, hours, availability, process.
  • Propose a privacy-conscious inquiry tracker using aggregate or minimal data.
  • Find friction points and rank fixes for V33 to approve.
D · Paid marketing

During the trial we prepare campaign hypotheses only: no ad spend and no paid-media management. Paid activity comes later, only after healthcare advertising rules, permissions and approvals are confirmed, and never with unsupported medical claims or patient stories used without lawful permission.

Roles, approvals and safeguards

How we work together.

On Call
  • Lead the digital audit, creative and marketing planning, and the reporting framework.
  • Develop the agreed trial deliverables and present a final roadmap.
  • Track scope, and get review and approval before any client-facing change.
V33 and its partners
  • Share links, insights, brand materials, approved clinic details and priorities.
  • Name one coordinating contact and a qualified clinical reviewer.
  • Give timely feedback and written approval for changes, medical content and any publishing.
  • Stay responsible for clinical care, patient records, appointments and business decisions.
Safeguards
  • No identifiable patient health records in marketing reports.
  • Consent-based, minimum-necessary contact data and secure access.
  • Egyptian healthcare advertising rules checked with qualified counsel before any promotion.
  • No testimonials, photos or clinical stories without consent and legal clearance.
  • Agreed support hours only. On Call is a name, not a 24/7 promise.
